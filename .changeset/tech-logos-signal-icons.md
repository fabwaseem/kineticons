Tech logos are a brand category of their own. The signal and Wi-Fi strength icons (`signal-zero`, `signal-low`, `signal-medium`, `signal-high`, `wifi-zero`, `wifi-low`, `wifi-high`) now draw their missing bars and arcs as dotted tracks, so each keeps the full shape at its real size instead of shrinking to a dot in a corner; the two "zero" icons blink their empty tracks in turn, as if searching.
