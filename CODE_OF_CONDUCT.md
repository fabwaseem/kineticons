# Code of conduct

Kineticons is a place to make good icons together. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome, whatever their
experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person, and take feedback the same way.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect people's time: stay on topic, and keep discussions in public threads where others can learn from them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, threats, and personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/fabwaseem). Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions,
or restrict anyone who does not follow this code, in issues, pull requests and any other project space.
